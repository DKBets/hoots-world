You don’t have to understand the whole path. You have to trust the One who sees it.

"Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and don’t lean on your own understanding. In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make your paths straight."

Your understanding runs out — his doesn’t. Trust him with the major, the money, the future, all of it, and he’ll straighten the road.
